What is a backdoor?

Backdoors are hidden methods set up to bypass normal security to give users or attakcers accsess to a computer systems, networks, or software. This makes it easy for hackers to keep a way into a system becuase they can easily hide it in the system and even when it gets reboted they can keep access so they don't have to go through firewalls or authentication processes.

How is this related to computer security?

When there are existing security problems on your computer or within your network it can be quite easy for someone with malicoius intent to get into your system and either install malicious software or to access and leak private information. This would be very bad for your security because it bypasses it with ease and makes it very hard to keep your information private. For places like companies and governments this is especiall bad.
