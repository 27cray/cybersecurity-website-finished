What is a Cracker?

Most people tend the think that hackers and crackers are the same thing, this is a common misconception. Crackers like hackers have a great ability to get through the security of a system, but crackers are the maliscious hackers who use their technical skills to break laws, steal data, disrupt operations, and commit fraud. Some common things crackers are very good at are password cracking, software cracking, and network/intrusions. They are often known as black hat hackers which means they have bad intent in mind.

How is this related to computer security?

This is related to computer security because these are the people who you have to worry about tyring to get into your computer and they are the ones that create security problems in the first place. These people try to attack systems and steal information that you don't want stolen. These are the people that would put backdoors into systems they want future access into and the people that would set up trojan-horse viruses along with many other forms of maleware and viruses.
