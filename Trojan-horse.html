What is a Trojan-horse Virus?

This is a tye of malware that disguises itself as legitimate software to trick users into downloading and running it. Trojans do not self replicate like some other viruses instead they are set up to rely on social engineering which persuades people into give scammers and crackers personal information or access to their system often without the user ever realizing that they are dealing with this problem. They do all of this by disguising their malicious code inside a normal looking program. Once downloaded or running the trojan acts in the background executing the additional hidden functions it is meant to do.

How is this related to computer security?

This is related to cybersecurity because these are hidden malware that aren't easy to spot unless you know how to find them and how to stay away from them. They use social engineering which makes it hard to know that your computer will be at risk because of these things. There are multipul types of trojan horses, some use backdoors or make backdoors, some target financial data (frequently used by scammers), some download and install things like ransomware and spyware, and others steal info. These are really importnat to be aware of becuase if you aren't aware of them it cna have big negative effects and you may have system damages and lose money due to these attacks. Some signs of these are unexplained computer slow downs, firewalls being disabled, strange pop-ups, and unknown programs running or changes to your settings you didn't personally make.
